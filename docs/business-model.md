# Business Model: Glass Workshop Scheduling and Logistics Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7315`
- ISCO-08: `7315`
- Occupation: Glass Makers, Cutters, Grinders and Finishers
- Social impact: worker-safety, product-quality, industrial-continuity

## Customer

- glass workshop operators / glassworks contractors
- independent glass-making, -cutting, -grinding and -finishing crews / crew cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- task/materials-usage/progress-record logging
- glass-materials supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to workshop safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a glass-forming/cutting-execution decision
  (e.g. deciding to proceed with a specific glass-forming or
  glass-cutting operation), ever
- no override of a workshop safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (burn hazard, cut hazard, furnace condition)
  always route to human sign-off, regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
